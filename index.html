The question it answers: does a Big Mac cost more or less in each country than in the US, once converted to dollars?

Tip: click the chart to zoom in.

Press Play under the chart, or drag the slider, to watch the ranking change from 2000 to 2026. Scroll inside the frame to see all of it. It needs an internet connection.
